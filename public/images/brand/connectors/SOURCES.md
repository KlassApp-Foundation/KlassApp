## WhatsApp

`whatsapp.svg` is the official WhatsApp glyph (green `#25D366`, white mark) already used by `resources/views/components/brand/whatsapp.blade.php`. Geometry is Meta's WhatsApp brand glyph.

Use follows the [WhatsApp brand guidelines](https://about.meta.com/brand/resources/whatsapp/whatsapp-brand). The mark is not recolored.

## Slack

`slack.svg` is the official 2019 Slack hash mark (aubergine `#E01E5A`, blue `#36C5F0`, green `#2EB67D`, yellow `#ECB22E`) already used by `resources/views/components/brand/slack.blade.php`.

Use follows the [Slack media kit](https://slack.com/intl/en-gb/media-kit). The mark is not recolored.
