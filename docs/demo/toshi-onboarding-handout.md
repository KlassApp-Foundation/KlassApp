# Set up a school from its checklist

**Toshi onboarding — one page for the demo**

You bring one file: the school's onboarding checklist. Toshi does the rest, with you approving each step.

## 1. Give Toshi the checklist

Open Toshi and click **Onboard from checklist**.

- **Upload** the `.xlsx` file, or
- **Connect Google Sheet** and pick the file in Google's picker. A Google Sheet is read as Excel; an `.xlsx` in Drive is read as it is.

Toshi asks you to agree to the data processing terms first. It reads nothing until you agree.

## 2. Toshi reads and checks it

Toshi counts what is in the file and shows a summary: learners, classes, streams, teachers, parents, subjects, terms.

Anything wrong is listed by **tab and row**, with what to fix. For example: "Learners, row 123: parent phone 12345." You can skip those rows or stop and fix the file.

## 3. You approve one step at a time

Toshi prepares one step, shows what it will create, and waits. Click the button — for example **Create 3 terms**, then **Create 8 classes and streams**, then **Create 5 subjects**, then **Create 8 teachers**, then **Create 120 learners**.

Each step is one transaction. If it fails, that step writes nothing and the steps you already approved stay.

## 4. Nothing is saved until you approve

Reading the file saves nothing. Typing a note saves nothing. A step is written only when you click its button.

## 5. Invites are prepared, not sent

At the end, Toshi says how many invites are ready — teachers and parents. **No email and no WhatsApp is sent.**

The privacy policy and terms apply.
