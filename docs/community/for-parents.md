# For parents

KlassApp reaches you on **WhatsApp** — the app already on most phones — so you don’t need another school login for everyday questions.

Built for real constraints: low bandwidth, everyday devices, and schools that need parents informed without forcing a new app install.

---

## How you get connected

1. Your child’s school uses KlassApp.  
2. You are linked as a parent (the school approves a link request, or staff add your number).  
3. You message the school’s WhatsApp business number and use the menu / short commands.

There is **no separate parent app required** for these WhatsApp flows. Some schools also offer a parent web portal after linking — ask your school what’s enabled.

---

## What you can ask (typical)

Exact wording depends on the school’s WhatsApp menu, but common asks include:

| Ask | Typical reply |
|---|---|
| **Fees** | Balances and what’s outstanding |
| **Grades / results** | Latest published marks |
| **Attendance** | Recent attendance summary |
| **Report** | Report-card PDF when the school has published one |

Messages are individual (not a parent group chat). Your number is not shown to other parents.

---

## Privacy & opt-out

- Schools control who is linked to which student.  
- You can ask the school to update your number if you change phones.  
- Opt-out / preference options are offered in the WhatsApp flow where enabled — ask the school if you need messages paused.
