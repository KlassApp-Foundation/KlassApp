# Early Adopters Program

An open education protocol for humans and agents.

Early adopters are contributors who test the protocol and shape its future. They run KlassApp with real classes, tell us where the product fails them, and decide with us what the public version must get right.

## Who it is for

Schools, and the people who run them. Nursery, primary, O level, and A level all belong, in any country. The right school is one that will actually use the product for marks, reports, and parent updates, and that can spare one person to speak for how the school works today.

Developers who want to contribute code start at [CONTRIBUTING.md](../../CONTRIBUTING.md).

## What schools get

Three months of the Growth plan.

During that time the school uses the hosted product: the onboarding Wizard or Toshi to set up, staff portals for day-to-day work, and WhatsApp for parents. We stay in contact for the whole three months.

## What we ask

- **A named champion.** One person at the school who can make time, answer questions, and speak for the staff. This is the person we write to and call.
- **A short onboarding interview.** Before or during setup, the champion walks us through how the school is organised: classes, terms, and who does what.
- **A 30-minute feedback call every two weeks.** What worked, what blocked the school, what should change before the next school arrives.
- **Sample report cards, and a description of how marks work today.** Real blank or anonymised examples are enough: the scale, the columns, how a term result is decided, and how parents receive it. We use these to check that the protocol can represent that school. Please remove student names and contact details before sending.

## Timeline

The Public Beta is **15 December 2026**. That is the date the early access page counts down to.

1. Apply on the early access page.
2. If we accept the school, we schedule the onboarding interview and name the champion.
3. The school sets up and uses KlassApp. The 30-minute call happens every two weeks from then through the Public Beta.

## How to apply

Apply at the early access page: [https://klassapp.xyz/early-access](https://klassapp.xyz/early-access).

The form asks for the school name, your name and role, email, phone, country, and roughly how many students you have. Use the champion's name if you already know who that will be. We reply by email to arrange the interview, and we confirm each place before the school is enrolled.
