## 4. The Problem / Why KlassApp

**Heading:** School software has forgotten the most important person.

**The old way (pain points):**
- Parents find out about exam results at the end of term — weeks after they're published
- Fee reminders get lost in WhatsApp group noise
- Attendance issues go unnoticed until it's too late
- Portals require smartphones, data, and patience

**The KlassApp way:**
- Grades are sent to parents the same day exams are marked — via WhatsApp
- Fee balances are a text away, with payment instructions included
- Absence detected → parent notified in minutes via WhatsApp, directly
- Works on any phone, any network, no app required. Just WhatsApp

---

## 5. How It Works

**Heading:** Three keywords. Complete visibility.

> Parents text a keyword to KlassApp on WhatsApp. Instantly, they get exactly what they need.

**Three cards:**
1. **📊 Exam Results** — Text `GRADES` to get formatted results for every child — scores, grades, class position
2. **💰 Fee Balance** — Text `FEES` for a real-time fee balance breakdown
3. **📅 Attendance** — Text `ATTENDANCE` for a term summary; schools can send automatic absence alerts

Plus: events, announcements, payment instructions, all on WhatsApp.

## 6. For Schools

**Features grid:**
