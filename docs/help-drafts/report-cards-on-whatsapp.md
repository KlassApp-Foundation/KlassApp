# Report cards on WhatsApp (draft for parents)

**Draft.** This guide is written ahead of delivery being approved. It is not yet a live promise to families.

## When you can see the report

Your child's school decides when a report is ready. Until the school releases the term, there is nothing to show — not in the app and not on WhatsApp. That is normal; it does not mean the report is lost.

## Ask for the report on WhatsApp

1. Open WhatsApp and message the KlassApp number your school gave you.
2. Send the word **REPORT**.
3. If a report has been released, you get it back as a PDF document. Tap it to open or save it.
4. If nothing has been released, the reply says so. Try again after the school tells you the reports are out.

Only the people linked to a child can get that child's report. The school links you; KlassApp does not add a parent on its own.

## See it in the app

1. Sign in with the email or phone number the school has for you.
2. Open **Marks** for your child.
3. The released term shows the marks and the report card. Before release, the page is empty.

If you have more than one child at the school, check the child's name at the top before you download anything.

## What Missing and ABS mean

- **Missing** — the school has no mark for that subject. It counts as 0 on the report.
- **ABS** — your child was absent for that subject. It also counts as 0.

Neither one is a mistake in the app. If you think a mark is wrong, ask the school; only the school can change it.

## Who to ask

1. **The school office or your child's class teacher** — for a mark, an absence, a missing report, or a change to your contact details.
2. **The school's report-card contact** — if the office cannot help, ask who owns report cards at the school.

KlassApp does not discuss a child's marks with anyone who is not linked to that child.

## If the PDF will not open

- Check your connection and try again.
- Save the file first, then open it from your files.
- If it still fails, ask the school to print or send a copy; the school has the same report.

## Your details

Keep your number and email up to date with the school. If your number changes, tell the school before reports are released, or the message will not reach you.
