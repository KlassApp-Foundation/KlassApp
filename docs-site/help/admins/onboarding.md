# Set up the school
After an invite, you choose the Wizard or Toshi. Both cover the same steps: school details, classes, subjects, teachers, students, terms, fees, WhatsApp, and plan.

## Choose a path
The choice page heading is **How would you like to set up** your school.

<Steps>
<Step title="Wizard">

Choose <Btn>Set up step by step</Btn>. This opens the Wizard. Use it when you already have the class list and the fee structure.

Guided chat stays off on this path. The panel can say **Coming soon** and **Meet Toshi**. That is expected.
</Step>
<Step title="Toshi">

Choose <Btn>Set up by chatting with Toshi</Btn>. Toshi, KlassApp's assistant, asks one question at a time and saves each answer. Guided setup does not need an AI key, and it does not turn the free-form assistant on.

You land on the dashboard with Toshi open. The checklist title is **Completing Setup**.
</Step>
</Steps>

You can change paths later. The dashboard banner is **Finish school setup**. **Set up manually** is always there. **Set up with Toshi** appears only while guided setup is on.

A school that signed up without an invite does not see this choice page. Use the same banner.

## Wizard steps
Choose <Btn>Continue →</Btn> after each step. On the review step choose <Btn>Confirm & finish</Btn>. **Teachers** and **Students** also offer <Btn>Skip for now</Btn>.

Steps every school sees, in order:

1. **School name**
2. **Approximate school size** — **Up to 500**, **Up to 1,000**, or **More than 1,000**
3. **Country**
4. **Board / Curriculum**
5. **Academic year**
6. **Structure & Class Teachers**
7. **Subjects**
8. **Teachers** (optional)
9. **Students** (optional)
10. **Academic terms** — three terms are filled in. Change the names and dates to match your school.
11. **Fee structures**
12. **WhatsApp verification**
13. **Plan selection**
14. Review, then <Btn>Confirm & finish</Btn>

A few extra steps appear only when your country or curriculum needs them: **School category**, **EMIS / Ministry code**, and **UNEB centre number**. If you do not see them, they do not apply. You can leave the exam centre number blank.

On **Teachers** and **Students** you can choose **Upload file**, **Paste names (one per line)**, **Add from paste**, or add one person. Students also have **Download template**.

When setup is done the screen says your school **is ready**. Choose <Btn>Go to dashboard</Btn>.

On the dashboard, <Btn>Continue setup</Btn> is the way back into the wizard. Edit School Details stays a settings page.

## After setup
In the menu, open <UiPath to="Settings" />. You can edit:

- **School details**
- **Academic years and terms**
- **Data exports**

**Settings** is only on the school admin menu. A head teacher does not see it.

## Add people next
[Add students](/help/add-students) from <UiPath to="Students" />. Parents then use [WhatsApp](/help/parents/whatsapp). Link requests land on [Link requests](/help/admins/link-requests).
