# Connect Google tools

::: info Coming soon
Google Drive, Google Classroom, and Google Sheets are not connected from the school admin screens on this release. There is no **Connect Google** button to use yet.
:::

## What you can do today
**Continue with Google** on the register page only creates a KlassApp login. It does not attach Drive, Classroom, or Sheets to the school.

To look at connections that do exist, open <UiPath to="Settings" /> and choose **Integrations** in the settings bar. **Settings** is on the school admin menu only.

If nothing is turned on for your school, the page says **No integrations available yet.**

WhatsApp is separate. Parents message the school's KlassApp number. See [WhatsApp for parents](/help/parents/whatsapp) and [Link requests](/help/admins/link-requests).

## What happens next
When a Google connection ships, this page will name the button and the menu path. Until then, do not expect Drive or Classroom inside Toshi.
