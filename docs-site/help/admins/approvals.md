# Review approvals
Staff leave, parent-link requests, lesson plans, and tasks wait here until you approve or reject them.

## Open the inbox
In the menu, choose <UiPath to="Approvals" />.

The heading is **Approvals**. The line under it is "Review and manage pending requests from staff."

Three counts sit at the top: **Pending**, **Approved**, and **Rejected**.

The table columns are **Type**, **Requester**, **Status**, **Comments**, **Requested**, and **Actions**.

An empty inbox says **No approval requests yet**.

## Act on a request
<Steps>
<Step title="Approve">

On a pending row, choose <Btn>Approve</Btn>. Confirm **Approve this request?**
</Step>
<Step title="Reject">

Choose <Btn>Reject</Btn>. The reason field says **Reason required...**. Choose <Btn>Confirm</Btn>.
</Step>
</Steps>

A parent-link row is labelled **Parent link (WhatsApp)**. Approving it links the number. Rejecting it tells the parent. You can also handle those cards on [Link requests](/help/admins/link-requests).

## What this inbox does not release
Marks are not released from this inbox. **Release marks** is a separate item under <UiPath to="Exams and reports > Release marks" />. The step-by-step marks guide is still a [placeholder](/help/marks).

::: info Note
Only a person at your school can approve a request for your school. Approving does not change another school's records.
:::
