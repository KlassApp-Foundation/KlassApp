# KlassApp for school admins
You run one school: people, classes, money, WhatsApp, and settings.

## Where you land
Sign in at the KlassApp login page. You open the school dashboard.

Until the academic year and classes exist, most pages send you back to finish setup. Follow [Onboarding paths](/help/admins/onboarding).

## Menu
The menu is grouped. Section names are **People**, **Academics**, **Money**, and **School**.

**People**

- **Students** — **All students**, **Admissions**, **Health records**
- **Teachers and staff**
- **Parents**

**Academics**

- **Classes and subjects** — **Classes and streams**, **Subjects**, **Timetable**, **Calendar**
- **Attendance**
- **Exams and reports** — **Exams and marks**, **Release marks**, **Grading**, **Report cards**
- **Library**

**Money**

- **Fees**
- **Unmatched payments**

**School**

- **WhatsApp**
- **Approvals**
- **Transport**
- **Settings** — **School details**, **Academic years and terms**, **Data exports**

**Settings** and **Classes and streams** are yours. A head teacher uses the same menu without those two rows. See [Head teacher](/help/head-teachers/).

## Common tasks
- Add a student: <UiPath to="Students" />, then <Btn>Add student</Btn> or <Btn>Import list</Btn>. See [Add students](/help/add-students).
- Edit the year and terms: <UiPath to="Settings > Academic years and terms" />.
- Fees: <UiPath to="Fees" />. **Send reminder** is on that page for a parent who still owes fees.
- Payments that did not match a student: <UiPath to="Unmatched payments" />.
- Parent WhatsApp links: <UiPath to="WhatsApp" />. The first section is **Link requests**. Approve, reject, or open **Approvals** when you must pick the student there.
- Other pending requests: <UiPath to="Approvals" />. That inbox is staff leave, parent links, lesson plans, and tasks.
- Connections: <UiPath to="Settings" />, then **Integrations**. Google Drive, Classroom, and Sheets are **Coming soon**. When no connection is turned on, the page says **No integrations available yet.**

Marks and report cards have menu entries. The step-by-step guides are still short placeholders: [Enter marks](/help/marks) and [Generate report cards](/help/generate-report-cards).
