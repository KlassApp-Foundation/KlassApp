# How marks are counted

This page explains what every mark on a mark sheet, a release sheet and a report card means, and how KlassApp turns marks into a **Total**, an **Aggregate (Agg)** and a **Division**. The rules are the same on every screen, so a learner's numbers on the release sheet always match their report card.

## The three things a subject can show

| What you see | What it means | Does it count? |
|---|---|---|
| A number, for example **78** | The mark the teacher entered, out of 100. | Yes, as that mark. |
| **–** (a dash) | No mark. The teacher left it blank or chose "No mark". | Yes, as **0**. |
| **Pending** | The subject's marks are not approved yet. | **No.** It is left out of Total, Aggregate and Division until it is approved. |

::: tip ABS is for attendance only
**ABS** means a learner was absent from roll call. It appears only in attendance. It never appears on mark sheets, release sheets or report cards. A learner who missed an exam has no mark for it, so the subject shows **–**.
:::

### Why a dash counts as 0

A blank mark is never hidden. If a dash quietly dropped out of the total, a learner with a missing subject could end up with a better total or a better division than a learner who sat every paper. Counting it as 0 keeps every learner on the same footing, and the dash makes the gap visible so a teacher can fix it before release.

### Why Pending does not count

Pending means the school has not checked those marks yet. Counting them would publish numbers nobody has approved. Once an admin approves the subject, Pending is replaced by the marks and the totals update.

## Total

The **Total** is the sum of the whole marks printed on the card.

- Each mark is rounded once, to a whole number (halves round up), and the printed number is the one that is added. You can always check a total by adding the numbers you see.
- A **–** adds 0.
- A **Pending** subject adds nothing.

Example: 78 + 85 + 70 + 90 = **323**.

## Grades and points

Each mark gets a grade from your school's grading scale (**Exams and reports > Grading**). Each grade carries points: fewer points is better.

The scale belongs to your school. For example, a school's scale might give 75 to 89 the grade C3 (3 points) and 65 to 74 the grade C4 (4 points). Check your own scale on the Grading page.

A **–** gets the lowest grade on your scale (for example F9, 9 points), because it counts as 0.

## Aggregate (Agg)

The **Aggregate** is the sum of the points of the subjects your school counts towards the division (for example English, Mathematics, Science and Social Studies).

Example: C3 (3) + C3 (3) + C4 (4) + D2 (2) = **Agg 12**.

A lower aggregate is a better result.

## Division

The **Division** comes from the aggregate, using your school's division table. For example:

| Aggregate | Division |
|---|---|
| up to 12 | 1 |
| 13 to 24 | 2 |
| 25 to 28 | 3 |
| 29 to 32 | 4 |
| 33 or more | U |

Your school can change these ranges on the Grading page. Division is not shown for classes that do not use one.

## A worked example

| Learner | Maths | English | Science | Social Studies | Total | Agg | Div |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Learner A | 78 (C3) | 85 (C3) | 70 (C4) | 90 (D2) | 323 | 12 | 1 |
| Learner B | – (F9) | 72 (C4) | 68 (C4) | 80 (C3) | 220 | 20 | 2 |

Learner B has no Maths mark. It shows as **–**, adds 0 to the total and 9 points to the aggregate.

## After release, a card does not change

When an admin releases marks, KlassApp stamps the release with the rules in force that day: the grading scale, the division table and the rule that a dash counts as 0. A released report card keeps those rules, even if the school changes its scale later. To change a released result, the marks must be corrected and released again.

## Where you see these rules

- **Teachers** see **–** on the marks entry screen for any learner without a mark, and a count of how many are left before submitting.
- **Admins** see Total, Agg and Div on the release sheet before releasing, and the same values on the Report cards page.
- **Parents and students** see the released Division, Aggregate and Total, the subject marks, and the report card. The WhatsApp message uses the same numbers.
