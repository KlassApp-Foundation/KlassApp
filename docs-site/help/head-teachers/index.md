# KlassApp for head teachers
You are the deputy admin for one school. You use the school admin menu, with two rows hidden.

The account card may say **School admin** unless a job title is saved on the profile. The difference is the menu, not that label.

## Where you land
Sign in at the KlassApp login page. You open your dashboard, then the same school pages a school admin uses for day-to-day work.

## Menu
You see the school admin groups: **People**, **Academics**, **Money**, and **School**.

You do **not** see:

- **Settings** (school details, academic years and terms, data exports, integrations)
- **Classes and streams** under **Classes and subjects**

**Classes and subjects** still opens. The first item you get is **Subjects**, then **Timetable** and **Calendar**.

Everything else matches the [school admin](/help/admins/) menu: **Students**, **Teachers and staff**, **Parents**, **Attendance**, **Exams and reports**, **Library**, **Fees**, **Unmatched payments**, **WhatsApp**, **Approvals**, and **Transport**.

Opening a settings address directly does not show the page.

## Common tasks
- Students, staff, and parents: under **People**.
- Subjects, the timetable, and the calendar: under **Classes and subjects**.
- Attendance for the school: **Attendance**.
- Exams, grading, and report cards: <UiPath to="Exams and reports" />. The written marks guide is still a [placeholder](/help/marks).
- Fees: **Fees**. **Send reminder** is on that page.
- Parent link requests: <UiPath to="WhatsApp" />, section **Link requests**.
- Leave, parent links, lesson plans, and tasks: <UiPath to="Approvals" />.

A class teacher is a different login. That person sees the teacher menu, not this one. See [Class teacher](/help/teachers/class).

## Get help
School details, the academic year, and integrations belong to the school admin. Product questions: [community@klassapp.xyz](mailto:community@klassapp.xyz)
