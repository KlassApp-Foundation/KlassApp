# Get started
Join KlassApp, then finish school setup with the Wizard or with Toshi, KlassApp's assistant.

## If you have an invite
Open the email. The button is **Set up your school**.

The page title is **Set up** and your school name. The email address is already filled in and cannot be changed.

<Steps>
<Step title="Create the school account">

Fill in **Your full name**, **Phone (WhatsApp)**, **Password**, and **Confirm password**. The password needs at least 8 characters.

Choose <Btn>Create school account</Btn>.
</Step>
<Step title="Choose how to finish setup">

The next page asks **How would you like to set up** your school.

Choose <Btn>Set up step by step</Btn> for the Wizard, or <Btn>Set up by chatting with Toshi</Btn> for guided setup.

Both paths cover the same steps. You can switch later from the dashboard.
</Step>
</Steps>

The link works once. If it has expired or was already used, the page says so. Ask for a new invite.

Read [Onboarding paths](/help/admins/onboarding) for the steps.

## If the sign-up page is open
Open [klassapp.xyz](https://klassapp.xyz) and go to register. The heading is **Create your KlassApp account**.

Enter **Your Full Name**, **Email Address**, and **Phone (WhatsApp)**. Set a password and confirm it. Tick **I agree to Terms and Conditions**.

Choose <Btn>Create account with password</Btn>, or <Btn>Continue with Google</Btn>. Google here only creates the login. It does not connect Google Drive or Classroom. That connection is [Coming soon](/help/admins/google).

## Confirm your email
The next page is **Check your email**.

Tap **Confirm email** in the message, or type the **6-digit code** and choose <Btn>Confirm email</Btn>.

No message? Check spam, then choose <Btn>Resend</Btn>.

You then land on the dashboard. A banner says **Finish school setup**. Choose **Set up manually** for the Wizard. **Set up with Toshi** shows only when guided setup is on for your school.

Schools that sign up this way do not see the invite choice page. Use the banner.

## If sign-up is closed
The register page shows **Request early access**. Send that form. When a platform admin approves it, you get the invite email above.

## What to have ready
School name, country, curriculum, and a class list help. Teachers and students can wait. The Wizard lets you skip those two steps.

## What happens next
[Set up the school](/help/admins/onboarding), then [add students](/help/add-students). Parents use [WhatsApp](/help/parents/whatsapp).
