# Sign up and confirm email
Create the school admin login, then confirm the email address. The full path, including invites, is [Get started](/help/getting-started).

## When sign-up is open
Open register on [klassapp.xyz](https://klassapp.xyz). The heading is **Create your KlassApp account**.

<Steps>
<Step title="Create the account">

Enter **Your Full Name**, **Email Address**, and **Phone (WhatsApp)**. Set a password and confirm it. Tick **I agree to Terms and Conditions**.

Choose <Btn>Create account with password</Btn>.

<Btn>Continue with Google</Btn> creates the same login with your Google account. It does not connect Google Drive or Classroom.
</Step>
<Step title="Confirm the email">

The next heading is **Check your email**. Tap **Confirm email** in the message, or enter the **6-digit code** and choose <Btn>Confirm email</Btn>.

No message? Check spam, then choose <Btn>Resend</Btn>.
</Step>
</Steps>

You land on the dashboard. Choose **Set up manually** on the **Finish school setup** banner, or follow [Onboarding paths](/help/admins/onboarding).

## When sign-up is closed
Register shows **Request early access**. Send the form. An invite email follows if a platform admin approves it. The button in that email is **Set up your school**.

::: tip
The phone field checks the number. If your number is rejected, say so on the early-access form.
:::
