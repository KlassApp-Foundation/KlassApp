# Sign up and confirm email
Create your school's KlassApp account. Your email address is the login.

## Steps
<Steps>
<Step title="Open the sign-up page">

Go to <Btn>app.klassapp.xyz</Btn> and choose sign up. Enter your name, email and a password.
</Step>
<Step title="Check your email">

KlassApp emails you a verification code. Type the code into the sign-up page.
</Step>
<Step title="You are in">

You now have an admin account for your own school. The next step is <a href="./setup-school">setting up the school</a>.
</Step>
</Steps>

::: tip
No confirmation email? Check spam, then use "resend" on the sign-up page.
:::

## What happens next
Set up the school: name, logo, academic year and terms.
