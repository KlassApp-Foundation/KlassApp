# KlassApp for bursars
You record fees and run payroll for one school. The account card says **Bursar**.

## Where you land
Sign in at the KlassApp login page. You open the bursar dashboard.

## Menu
In order:

- **Dashboard**
- **Fees & Payments**
- **Data Exports**
- **Holidays**
- **Payroll** — **Templates**, **Salaries**, **Payslips**, **Batch Run**

You do not see **Students**, **WhatsApp**, or **Settings**. Those stay with the [school admin](/help/admins/).

## Common tasks
- See payments and what the school is still owed: **Fees & Payments**.
- Record a payment: <Btn>+ Record Payment</Btn>. The form heading is **Record Payment**. Choose <Btn>Record Payment</Btn> to save.
- Download figures: **Data Exports**.
- School holidays: **Holidays**.
- Pay: open **Payroll**, then the template, the salaries, the payslips, or a **Batch Run**.

Fee reminders that parents get from the school admin **Fees** page are not a separate bursar menu item. Parents can still ask **Fee Balance** on WhatsApp. See [Use KlassApp on WhatsApp](/help/parents/whatsapp).

## Get help
Ask the school admin for a student who is missing from the fee list. Product questions: [community@klassapp.xyz](mailto:community@klassapp.xyz)
