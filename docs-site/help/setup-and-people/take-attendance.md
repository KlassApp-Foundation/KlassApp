# Take attendance in one tap

The register lists every learner as **Present** before you touch it. Tap **Absent** for the learners who are not there, add a comment if you want one, and save once.

## Open the register

1. Open **Attendance**.
2. Press **Take attendance**.
3. Choose the class or stream. A class teacher sees **Whole class**; everyone else sees **All my streams** and the streams the school lets them mark.
4. Choose the **Date** and the **Session** (**Morning** or **Afternoon**).
5. Press **Open register**.

The teacher dashboard also opens this register directly.

## Mark the register

- Everyone starts as **Present**.
- Tap **Absent** on a learner to change it. The row turns and a comment box appears.
- Type a short comment in the box if the absence needs one. The comment is stored with that learner's attendance and is shown to the parent in the weekly summary.
- To put a learner back, tap **Present**.
- Press **Save attendance** once, at the bottom.

The page says it plainly: "Everyone is present unless you tap Absent. Save once." Nothing is saved until you press **Save attendance**.

A learner who already has a saved row for that date and session opens with the saved status and comment. Saving again updates those rows; it does not create a second register.

## Who can mark which register

- **Class teacher:** every active stream of the class they head.
- **Everyone else:** only the streams the school's attendance scope allows — their own stream, or the streams they teach.
- The same check runs again when you save, and every learner on the posted register must belong to that roster.

If you cannot see a class, the school has not given you that scope. Ask the school admin; do not ask another teacher to mark it for you.

## If the year is not set up

The page says **Set up the academic year first** when there is no current academic year or class. The school admin sets the year; attendance cannot be marked until then.

## After saving

The attendance history is under **Attendance history**. A saved register can be corrected by reopening the same date and session, changing the status, and saving again.
