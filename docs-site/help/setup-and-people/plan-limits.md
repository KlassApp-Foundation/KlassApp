# Plan limits

Your plan sets how many teachers and students the school can have at once. A limit of 0 means unlimited, which is how the founding-school plan works.

## Where to see your plan

Open **Settings**, then **Plan & subscription** (`/admin/subscriptions`). The page says **Your current plan is NAME**, and lists the subscription history.

Administrator seats count toward the same people limit as teachers.

## What happens when you reach a limit

### Adding one person

The add form stops before it saves and shows:

> Your PLAN plan allows N teachers. You already have M. Talk to sales to upgrade and add more.

The same message appears for students. The person is not created.

### Importing a list

An import fills the free places and reports the rest. Nothing is half-created:

> Imported N teachers. M teachers were not imported: your PLAN plan allows X teachers and the school now has Y. Talk to sales to upgrade and add more.

The same wording is used for students. Fix the count or upgrade, then import the remaining rows.

## What a limit does not block

- It does not delete or deactivate anyone already on the school.
- It does not stop marks, attendance, report cards or messaging.
- It does not stop an admission that was already approved and paid; the school gets a notice instead of a block.

## What to do

1. Read the message. It names the plan, the limit and the current count.
2. Remove or deactivate people you no longer need, if that is right for the school.
3. If the school needs more, talk to sales about the next plan, then try again.

## Who can see this

A school admin. A teacher, parent or student does not see the plan page. The superadmin can see the same limits on the school's detail page.
