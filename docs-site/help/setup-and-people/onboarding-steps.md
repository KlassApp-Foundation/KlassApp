# School setup steps and the checklist

The setup wizard walks through one list of steps. Toshi shows the same list as a checklist. Both read the same source, so the counts and the step names always agree.

## The wizard

Open the wizard from the setup link you get after signup. The address carries the step, for example `/admin/onboarding/wizard?step=academic_year`, and it changes as you move, so a refresh keeps your place.

Across the top of the step card:

- **Step X of Y** — the same total the side panel uses.
- **Optional** — shown on a step you may skip.

Under the card:

- **Continue →** moves forward.
- **← Previous** goes back.
- **Save & return** appears when you edit a step from the review screen.
- **Cancel edit** appears in the same case.
- **Confirm & finish** appears on the last step, **Review & confirm**.

## The side panel

- **Setup X of Y** — how many steps are done.
- **Now: STEP NAME** — the step on the screen, not the first unfinished one.
- **Jump to** — pick any step by name; finished steps are marked **(done)**.
- **Nothing saved yet.** — shown before the first save. After that, the panel lists what is set up.

## The steps, in order

1. School name
2. Approximate school size
3. Country
4. Board / Curriculum
5. School category
6. EMIS / Ministry code
7. UNEB centre number
8. Academic year
9. Structure & Class Teachers
10. Subjects
11. Teachers
12. Students
13. Academic terms
14. Fee structures
15. WhatsApp verification
16. Plan selection
17. Review & confirm

**Structure & Class Teachers** is where you can add a stream and invite a class teacher. Both are optional; neither stops **Continue →**.

## The Toshi checklist

Open Toshi and its setup checklist shows:

- **Progress — X/Y** at the top.
- One row per step. A finished row says it is **set up already, review it**; an unfinished row says **not set up yet, start it**.
- **Setup X of Y** in the collapsed summary, with the same **Now:** label as the wizard.

Pressing a row opens that step. The checklist does not have its own list; it cannot drift from the wizard.

## Leaving and coming back

- A saved step stays saved. Closing the browser and returning lands on the step from the address.
- The wizard does not auto-finish when the last step is saved. You land on **Review & confirm**, read the summary and press **Confirm & finish**.
- After finishing, the checklist stays available so you can go back to any step.

## If a count looks wrong

The card, the side panel and the address are built from the same list. If they disagree, note the school, the step name and the numbers, and report it; do not try to fix it by jumping between steps.
