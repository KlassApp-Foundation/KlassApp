# KlassApp for parents
You follow your children. Most answers also work on WhatsApp, with no extra app.

## Where you land
Sign in at the KlassApp login page with the email and password the school gave you. You can also open the **Dashboard** link the school's KlassApp number sends on WhatsApp.

You need at least one child linked to your account. Linking is [Use KlassApp on WhatsApp](/help/parents/whatsapp).

## Menu
In order:

- **Dashboard**
- **Children**
- **Fees**
- **Grades**
- **Attendance**

With one linked child, **Fees**, **Grades**, and **Attendance** open that child. With more than one, those items open **Children** so you pick who you mean.

## Common tasks
- See who is linked: **Children**.
- What is still owed: **Fees**.
- Latest scores: **Grades**.
- Presence: **Attendance**.

These pages are for reading. You do not add students or change marks here.

On WhatsApp, **Open menu** lists **Fee Balance**, **Exam Results**, **Attendance**, **Report Card**, **Help**, **Demo**, and **Dashboard**. You can also type `fees`, `marks`, `grades`, `attendance`, or `report`.

## Get help
Ask the school admin if a child is missing or a link request is still waiting. The school approves that on **WhatsApp** or **Approvals**. Product questions: [community@klassapp.xyz](mailto:community@klassapp.xyz)
