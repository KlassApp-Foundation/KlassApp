# KlassApp for subject teachers
You teach one or more subjects. You do not automatically get exams and report cards for a whole stream.

If the school also names you as the class teacher of a stream, use [Class teacher](/help/teachers/class). The login is the same. The menu grows.

## Menu
When you are not a class teacher, the menu is:

- **Dashboard**
- **Classes**
- **Timetable**
- **Attendance**
- **Homework**
- **Marks**
- **Notices**
- **Events**

**Exams**, **Report Cards**, and **Class Streams** stay hidden.

## Common tasks
- Your classes: **Classes**.
- Your week: **Timetable**.
- Register, when the school expects you to mark it: **Attendance**.
- Work you set: **Homework**.
- Marks for an exam assigned to you: **Marks**. You can enter marks on exams you own. The written guide is still a [placeholder](/help/marks).
- Staff messages and dates: **Notices** and **Events**.

You cannot open another teacher's exam from the marks address. The page refuses it.

## Get help
Ask the school admin if a class or an exam is missing. Product questions: [community@klassapp.xyz](mailto:community@klassapp.xyz)
