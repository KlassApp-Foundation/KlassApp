# KlassApp for class teachers
You are the homeroom teacher for a stream. You see the teacher menu, plus exams and report cards for that stream.

The school sets this on the class, not as a separate account type. If you also teach a subject, you still have this same login.

## Menu
In order:

- **Dashboard**
- **Classes**
- **Timetable**
- **Attendance**
- **Exams**
- **Homework**
- **Marks**
- **Report Cards**
- **Class Streams** — when you are the teacher for that stream
- **Notices**
- **Events**

**Exams** and **Report Cards** show because you are the class teacher. A subject teacher without a stream does not see them.

If you are the class teacher of a stream but not the homeroom on the class link, you see **Class Streams** and you do not see **Report Cards**.

## Common tasks
- Who is in the stream: **Classes**.
- Your week: **Timetable**.
- Today's register: **Attendance**. The attendance how-to is not written yet.
- An exam for your stream: **Exams**.
- Work for the class: **Homework**.
- Marks for an exam you own, or for your stream: **Marks**. The step-by-step marks guide is still a [placeholder](/help/marks).
- Report cards for your homeroom class: **Report Cards**. That guide is also a [placeholder](/help/generate-report-cards).
- Stream exams and marks: **Class Streams**.
- Messages and dates: **Notices** and **Events**.

A teacher whose profile includes the principal designation can approve lesson plans and assignments. A teacher with the leave-checker designation can approve leave. Those duties sit on this same teacher login. They are not the [head teacher](/help/head-teachers/) account.

## Get help
Ask your school admin to assign the stream if **Exams** or **Report Cards** are missing. Product questions: [community@klassapp.xyz](mailto:community@klassapp.xyz)
