# KlassApp for students
You see your own classes, marks, and messages. You do not see another student's record.

## Where you land
Sign in at the KlassApp login page. You open the student dashboard.

## Menu
In order:

- **Dashboard**
- **Marks**
- **Attendance**
- **Homework**
- **Assignments**
- **Events**
- **Notices**
- **Library**
- **Holidays**
- **Chats**
- **Activity**

There is no separate timetable item. Ask your teacher, or check **Events**.

## Common tasks
- Scores: **Marks**. Only your marks. The way teachers enter them is still a [placeholder](/help/marks) for staff.
- Presence: **Attendance**.
- Work to do: **Homework** and **Assignments**.
- Dates and messages from the school: **Events**, **Notices**, and **Holidays**.
- Library activity: **Library**.
- Messages with staff: **Chats**.
- What you did in KlassApp: **Activity**.

Parents have their own login and their own WhatsApp chat. They do not use this menu.

## Get help
Ask your class teacher or the school admin if a subject or a mark is missing. Product questions go through the school.
