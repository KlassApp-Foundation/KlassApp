# Parent walkthrough

**Draft until the stable release.** Demo school only. The learner and parent names are invented.

## Step 1 — Sign in

Sign in with the email or phone number the school has for you.

**You should see** the parent dashboard. If more than one child is linked, each child has a card.

<img src="/walkthrough/parent-01-dashboard-375.png" alt="Parent dashboard at 375 px" style="max-width:375px;border:1px solid #e2e8f0;border-radius:8px" />
<img src="/walkthrough/parent-01-dashboard-1280.png" alt="Parent dashboard at 1280 px" style="max-width:100%;border:1px solid #e2e8f0;border-radius:8px" />

## Step 2 — Open your child

Open **Children**. Each linked child has **Dashboard**, **Marks**, **Fees** and **Attendance**.

**You should see** only the children linked to you. If a child is missing, ask the school to link them: send the child's KlassApp number to the school, or ask the office to use the link-request form.

<img src="/walkthrough/parent-02-children-375.png" alt="Children at 375 px" style="max-width:375px;border:1px solid #e2e8f0;border-radius:8px" />
<img src="/walkthrough/parent-02-children-1280.png" alt="Children at 1280 px" style="max-width:100%;border:1px solid #e2e8f0;border-radius:8px" />

## Step 3 — Open Marks

Press **Marks** under your child.

**You should see** the released terms. **Missing** means the school has no mark for that subject; **ABS** means your child was absent. Both count as zero. Before a term is released, nothing is shown — that is normal.

<img src="/walkthrough/parent-03-marks-375.png" alt="Parent marks at 375 px" style="max-width:375px;border:1px solid #e2e8f0;border-radius:8px" />
<img src="/walkthrough/parent-03-marks-1280.png" alt="Parent marks at 1280 px" style="max-width:100%;border:1px solid #e2e8f0;border-radius:8px" />

## Step 4 — Download the report card

Press **Download latest report card**.

**You should see** one PDF for your child only. Open it, or save it to your phone.

<img src="/walkthrough/parent-04-card-375.png" alt="Report card at 375 px" style="max-width:375px;border:1px solid #e2e8f0;border-radius:8px" />
<img src="/walkthrough/parent-04-card-1280.png" alt="Report card at desktop width" style="max-width:100%;border:1px solid #e2e8f0;border-radius:8px" />

## On WhatsApp

Message the school's KlassApp number with **REPORT**. If the school has released a report, you get it back as a PDF. If not, the reply says nothing has been released yet. The card is for your child only.

## If something looks wrong

Ask the school office or your child's class teacher. Only the school can change a mark, an absence or a link. KlassApp support cannot discuss a child's marks with anyone who is not linked to that child.
