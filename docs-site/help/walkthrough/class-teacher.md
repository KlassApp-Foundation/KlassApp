# Class teacher walkthrough

**Draft until the stable release.** Demo school only.

You are the class teacher for the class. You enter or check marks, approve them, and print the class report.

## Step 1 — Sign in

Sign in with the class teacher's email and password.

**You should see** the teacher dashboard, with the class you head named at the top.

<img src="/walkthrough/teacher-01-dashboard-1280.png" alt="Teacher dashboard at 1280 px" style="max-width:100%;border:1px solid #e2e8f0;border-radius:8px" />

## Step 2 — Open your marks

In the sidebar, open **Marks**.

**You should see** the exams you can mark, with **Enter marks** or **Continue**. A returned exam shows the reviewer's note.

<img src="/walkthrough/teacher-04-marks-list-375.png" alt="Teacher marks list at 375 px" style="max-width:375px;border:1px solid #e2e8f0;border-radius:8px" />
<img src="/walkthrough/teacher-04-marks-list-1280.png" alt="Teacher marks list at 1280 px" style="max-width:100%;border:1px solid #e2e8f0;border-radius:8px" />

## Step 3 — Enter marks, ABS, and a blank

Open **Enter marks**. Each learner is a card with a mark field and an **ABS** button.

- Type the mark, or press **ABS** when the learner was absent.
- Press **Save draft** as you go. A row reads **Saved** only after the server confirms it.
- Leave one mark blank on purpose. **Submit** stays off and the page says how many learners still need a mark or ABS.
- Fill it or mark it **ABS**, save, then press **Submit**.

A submitted subject cannot be edited until it is returned.

## Step 4 — Approve or return

In the sidebar, open **Review marks**.

**You should see** only the classes you head. Press **Approve**, or return the subject with a reason of at least 10 characters. The teacher sees the note.

<img src="/walkthrough/teacher-02-review-375.png" alt="Review marks at 375 px" style="max-width:375px;border:1px solid #e2e8f0;border-radius:8px" />
<img src="/walkthrough/teacher-02-review-1280.png" alt="Review marks at 1280 px" style="max-width:100%;border:1px solid #e2e8f0;border-radius:8px" />

## Step 5 — Open the class report

In the sidebar, open **Report Cards**.

**You should see** the classes and streams you head. Open the class to print a card for one learner or the whole class.

<img src="/walkthrough/teacher-03-report-cards-375.png" alt="Teacher report cards at 375 px" style="max-width:375px;border:1px solid #e2e8f0;border-radius:8px" />
<img src="/walkthrough/teacher-03-report-cards-1280.png" alt="Teacher report cards at 1280 px" style="max-width:100%;border:1px solid #e2e8f0;border-radius:8px" />

## What a class teacher cannot do

A class teacher does not release marks on **Marks Release** in this build. A school admin or the class teacher's release screen handles that; a subject teacher who does not head the class sees no **Review marks** at all.
