# KlassApp for platform admins
You look after every school on this KlassApp instance. You do not run one school's day-to-day classes.

## Where you land
Sign in at the KlassApp login page. You open the platform dashboard.

## Menu
The side menu, in order:

- **Dashboard**
- **Schools**
- **Subscriptions**
- **Plans**
- **Reports**
- **Mail List**
- **Settings**

## Common tasks
- **Schools** lists the schools on this instance. Open a school to see its record and its users.
- **Subscriptions** and **Plans** are the plan catalogue and which school is on which plan.
- **Reports** is the platform report list, including contact and demo requests.
- **Mail List** is the mailing-list subscribers.
- **Settings** holds platform settings, including co-admins, features, and locations.

Early-access requests are not in that side menu. Open `/platform/early-access` while signed in as a platform admin. The table has an **Invite** column. Choose <Btn>Invite</Btn> to email **Set up your school**. Later buttons on that row are <Btn>Resend</Btn> and <Btn>Revoke</Btn>. Anyone who is not a platform admin gets an error on that page.

## What you do not see
You do not get the school menu (**Students**, **Fees**, **WhatsApp**, and the rest). A school admin does that work inside one school. See [School admin](/help/admins/).
