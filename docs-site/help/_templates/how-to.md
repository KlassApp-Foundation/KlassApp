# Mark today's attendance
Record who is present in your stream. Parents of absent students get a WhatsApp message.

## Before you start
You must be the class teacher of the stream.

## Steps
<Steps>
<Step title="Open Attendance">

In the menu, choose <UiPath to="Attendance > Mark today" />.

<Shot src="/docs-preview/img/teacher-attendance_2026-09-29.webp" alt="Attendance page with Mark today highlighted" url="app.klassapp.xyz/teacher/attendance" :w="1280" :h="720" caption="Desktop 1280 × 720 · demo school">
  <Mark box :x="1.5" :y="26" :w="19" :h="9" /><Mark :n="1" :x="21" :y="26" />
</Shot>

1. Attendance in the menu
</Step>
<Step title="Save">

Tap <Btn>Save attendance</Btn>. You'll see "Attendance saved for P.5 Blue".
</Step>
</Steps>

::: tip
Mark attendance before 9:00 so parents get the message the same morning.
:::

## What happens next
Parents of absent students get one WhatsApp message.
