# Take attendance for a class or a stream

Who: teachers and school admins.
Where: **Take attendance**, or open `/teacher/attendance/register` (admins: `/admin/attendance/register`).

The page opens on today. Everyone on the register is marked **Present** already. You only change the ones who are not there.

## Steps

1. Open **Take attendance** from your dashboard or the menu.
2. At the top, choose the **class**.
3. Choose the **stream**. A class head can pick the whole class; a subject teacher sees the streams they teach.
4. Check the **date** and **session** (forenoon or afternoon). The date cannot be in the future or before the academic year starts.
5. Click **Mark all present** if you want to start from a clean "everyone here".
6. For each student who is absent, tap **Absent** on their row. Add a comment if the school asks for one.
7. Click **Save attendance** once.
8. Wait for the green line: it says how many rows were saved. The page does not reload.

Above the button, the page tells you what **Mark all present** will change: "Sets every student in this class" or "Sets every student on this register. Other streams stay unchanged until you open them."

## What to know

- **Nothing is saved until you click Save attendance.** Changing a radio button does not save.
- **Save once.** Saving the same register twice in a row is a known risk; wait for the green line before you leave or save again.
- A class head's register covers every stream in the class. A stream teacher's register covers that stream only. The page says which one you are looking at.
- The **Open register** button at the top applies the class, stream, date and session you chose.
- If a student is missing from the list, the class or stream is wrong, or the student is not active. Check **Students** first.
