# Onboarding with Toshi

> **Proof of concept: behind a per-school flag.** This is not on for every school. A school sees it only when the flag is on.

Toshi sets a school up from its checklist workbook. You approve every step; nothing is written until you do.

## 1. Start

On the dashboard, click **Set up with Toshi**. The Toshi panel opens with a setup card.

## 2. The agreement

The first card is **Agreement**. It says the checklist holds children's names, classes and parent phone numbers, and that nothing is saved until you approve each step.

Tick **I agree on behalf of the school**, then click **Continue**. Toshi does not read the file before this.

## 3. Give Toshi the workbook

The card asks for the checklist.

- **Upload** the `.xlsx` file, or
- click **Choose from Google Drive** and pick it in Google's picker.

Then click **Read this checklist**.

## 4. Read and checked

Toshi reads the file and shows a summary: learners, classes, streams, teachers, parents, subjects and terms.

Problems are listed by **tab and row**, with what to fix. For example, "Learners, row 123: parent phone 12345". You can:

- **Skip these rows** and continue, or
- stop and fix the workbook, then read it again.

Reading saves nothing.

## 5. One step at a time

Toshi prepares the first step — academic terms — and shows what it will create. Each step has:

- the main button, which names the outcome (for example **Create 3 terms**);
- **Change**, to keep the sheet as written or type a note (a note writes nothing);
- **Skip**, to leave that step unwritten.

Click the main button to approve. Toshi writes that step, says what was created, links to the page, and moves to the next step.

The steps are: academic terms, classes and streams, subjects, teachers and teaching assignments, learners and parents.

Each step is one transaction. If it fails, that step writes nothing and the steps you already approved stay.

## 6. Done

The last card is **Done**. It says:

- how many invites are ready — teachers and parents;
- that **nothing was sent**: no email, no WhatsApp;
- that KLS numbers are present and phone numbers are in international format;
- that class teachers are listed but not written.

Click **Set up from checklist** to run another workbook.

## What is not available yet

- Writing class teachers (they are listed only).
- Sending the prepared invites.
- Exams, fees and SchoolPay during onboarding.
- A **Back** button between steps; correct a step with **Change**, or stop and read the workbook again.
- Using it without the per-school flag.
