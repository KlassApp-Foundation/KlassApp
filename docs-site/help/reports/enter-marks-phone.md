# Enter marks on a phone

Marks entry works as cards on a phone: one learner at a time, saved as you go. A teacher can finish a class while walking between rooms, and the sheet tells you what is still missing before you submit.

## Open the sheet

1. Open **Marks**.
2. Press **Enter marks**, or **Continue** for a sheet you started.

A school admin or head teacher uses the same screen for any class in the school.

## Fill the sheet

- Each card is one learner.
- Type the mark, or press **ABS** when the learner was absent. Typing **ABS** or **AB** also records an absence.
- Press **Save draft** when you stop.
- **Next** and **Previous** move between learners and keep the cursor in a mark field; they name the learner.

## Saved, not saved, offline

- A mark reads **Saved** only after the server confirms it.
- Until then it reads **Not saved yet**.
- If the connection drops, keep typing. The typed mark stays **Not saved yet**, and the screen saves it when the connection returns. Check the row says **Saved** before you leave.

## Submit

- **Submit** stays off until every learner has a saved mark or an ABS.
- The screen says how many are missing: "A mark or ABS is missing for N learners."
- After submit, the sheet shows **ABS**, not a zero and not "AB".
- A blank on this sheet is not labelled Missing. **Missing** is the word on the mark sheet and on the report after the set is released.

## What the school tells teachers

Set **ABS** for an absence before you submit. Do not leave the cell blank and do not type 0 for an absence: 0 is a mark, ABS is an absence, and both appear differently on the report.

## Who can do what

- A subject teacher enters, saves and submits their own subjects.
- A class teacher does the same for their class, and can approve or return on **Review marks**.
- A school admin or head teacher can enter, save and submit for any class in their own school.

## Parents

Parents see nothing until the set is released. See [Approvals and release](/help/reports/approvals-and-release).
