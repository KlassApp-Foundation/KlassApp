# Governance

::: info Planned: details to be confirmed
This page states the principle only. The details below are not settled yet.
:::

At KlassApp's full launch, founding early adopter schools join a governing council. They sit alongside KlassApp Foundation members and the product and technical team. The spirit is the same as community-governed open-source and web3 projects: the people who use the project help guide it.

## Who sits on the council

- Founding early adopter schools
- KlassApp Foundation members
- The product and technical team

## A voice in direction

The council gives schools a voice in direction and priorities.

Voting rules, the number of seats, the legal structure, and token mechanics are to be defined with the founding schools.

## The source is public

The source is public on GitHub; supported self-hosting opens after an independent security review.

The repository is [KlassApp on GitHub](https://github.com/KlassApp-Foundation/KlassApp).

Schools enter through the [Early Adopters Program](/community/early-adopters).
