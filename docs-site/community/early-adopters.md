# Early Adopters Program

Early adopters are schools that run KlassApp with real classes before the Public Beta, tell us where it fails them, and decide with us what the public version must get right.

## Who it is for

Schools from nursery to secondary, in any country, and the people who run them. The right school will actually use KlassApp for marks, reports and parent updates, and can spare one person to speak for how the school works today.

::: tip Developers
Want to contribute code instead? Start with [Contributing](/community/contributing).
:::

## What schools get

Three months of the Growth plan.

During that time the school uses the hosted product: the onboarding Wizard or Toshi to set up, staff portals for day-to-day work, and WhatsApp for parents. We stay in contact for the whole three months.

## What we ask

- **A named champion.** One person at the school who can make time, answer questions and speak for the staff. This is the person we write to and call.
- **A short onboarding interview.** Before or during setup, the champion walks us through how the school is organised: classes, terms, and who does what.
- **A 30-minute feedback call every two weeks.** What worked, what blocked the school, and what should change before the next school arrives.
- **Sample report cards, and a description of how marks work today.** Blank or anonymised examples are enough: the scale, the columns, how a term result is decided, and how parents receive it.

::: warning
Remove student names and contact details from any sample before you send it.
:::

## How to apply

Apply at [klassapp.xyz/early-access](https://klassapp.xyz/early-access).

The form asks for the school name, your name and role, email, phone, country, and roughly how many students you have. Use the champion's name if you already know who that will be. We reply by email to arrange the interview, and we confirm each place before the school is enrolled.
