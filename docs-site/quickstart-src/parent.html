KlassApp quick start

Everything comes to your WhatsApp. No app to install.

For parents
1

Get joined

Your school adds your phone number. Save the school's KlassApp number to your contacts so messages reach you.

2

Get the report card

Send report to the school's KlassApp number. If you have more than one child, reply with the number next to your child's name. The PDF works for 7 days.

3

Know why your child was absent

When a teacher marks your child absent, you get a WhatsApp message the same morning.

4

Know what you owe

The bursar can send a fee reminder. Each message shows only your own child's balance — nobody else's.

5

Get help

Reply help to the school's KlassApp number to see everything you can ask for.

6

Keep your number

KlassApp uses your phone number to link you to your child. Tell the school office if you change it.

Save the number

The school's KlassApp number is on the school noticeboard. Saving it once means the report card lands in the right chat.

Privacy

Your child's marks and your phone number stay between you and the school. Read more: klassapp.xyz/privacy
