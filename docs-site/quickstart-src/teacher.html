KlassApp quick start

Mark attendance and enter marks for your class.

For teachers
1

Log in

Open app.klassapp.xyz on your phone or computer. Your school office sends you a link by email when they invite you — it works for 72 hours.

2

Mark attendance

Menu, then Attendance. Everyone starts as present. Tap a name to change it to Absent or Late, then save. Parents of absent students get a WhatsApp message.

3

Enter marks

Menu, then Exams & Marks. Choose the exam, your class and subject, then enter each student's marks.

4

See your students

Menu, then Students. Check names, attendance and marks for your class.

5

Missing marks

The headteacher's Report Cards page shows a Missing Marks Report — check it before results are published.

6

Need help?

Ask your school office first. The full guide is at Menu, then Help & Docs.

Remember

Save attendance before you leave the page. Saved attendance is what parents see.

WhatsApp

Your school's KlassApp number is on the school noticeboard. Teachers can send how-to texts, like the ones in the full guide.
