Biggest undesigned areas, ranked by user impact

  1. Marks entry (admin/marks/*, 16 files; teacher/marks/*). It's used by every teacher every term, and a mistake reaches report cards and parents. Designed below.
  2. Parent dashboard and child pages (parent/*). It's the largest audience and it's mobile-first, yet it's the thinnest views in the repo (five files, 881–5,226 bytes). Designed below.
  3. Timetable (admin/timetable, plus the new teacher/timetable/{index,form,manage} from this sync). It was just built with no design pass, so this is the cheapest moment to align it. Designed below.
  4. Printed documents: report card PDFs, ID card, bus pass, payslip. Only the greys and radii were fixed; the layouts have never been designed.
  5. WhatsApp and SMS message copy. The templates live in code, not views, and have never had a copy pass beyond the report card.
  6. Admin people pages: admin/member, staff, parent and teacher show, 15–25 KB each and all legacy. Profile photos are done; the rest isn't.
  7. Accountant: fees, payments and payroll (19 files). This is where the bursar lives; the C1 bursar list covers the student side only.
  8. Library, reception and the staff small modules. They're low traffic, so do them last.

1 · Marks entry grid

One stream × one exam × the teacher's own subjects. It works keyboard-first: Enter moves down, Tab moves right, and the grade computes live from admin/school/grades. Each cell autosaves on blur, with a quiet status line and no toast per cell. A value outside 0 to the exam's max is flagged inline and not saved. A subject teacher sees only their subject columns, as in the C1 matrix. Every input is 44px tall.

3 · Timetable week

It reads as a grid of periods × days. A teacher sees their own lessons plus free periods; an admin sees one stream and can edit. A clash (the same teacher in two streams at once) is amber with a text label, never colour alone. Below 760px it becomes a one-day list with day tabs; the 720px grid shown here scrolls horizontally inside its frame.

Later: configurable admission form builder (sketch only, not built)

Admin → Settings → Admission form. The Ugandan field set above is the default template, and schools start from it. Each field can be shown or hidden, made optional or required, and dragged to reorder within its step. Schools can add custom fields (short text, long text, choice, date, file). Rules can apply per class, for example "required for S.1 only" or "hidden for nursery". Three limits come with it. Protected fields (name, sex, date of birth, class, primary phone) can't be hidden. Health fields can't be made required and never enter list views or exports. Tribe, community, national ID and income can't be added as custom fields.
