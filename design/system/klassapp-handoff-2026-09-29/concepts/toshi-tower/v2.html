Review notes — not yet handed off

What changed from v1

  • Protocol layer removed. #protocol tells that story; the tower is now two blocks, KlassApp core and Toshi.
  • Six channels in, three roles out, matching the landing's own copy and connector set. Everything connects as a mesh around the core, rather than a one-way pipeline.
  • Glow language, not flat. The container, mist and dot grid, node cards, connector gradients, dashed flow streaks, bloom and breathing all reuse the values in landing-preview.css as they are.
  • Toshi is green: the .hub-mark gradient #2DD46A → #22C55E → #16A34A. There is no clay anywhere.

Tokens — landing names only

The only custom properties used are --brand-*, --text-*, --border, --violet-accent and --font-*, copied from landing-preview.css :root. There is no --d-* or --toshi-* anywhere in this file.

Literals are used only where the landing CSS itself uses literals: #2DD46A and #16A34A (from .hub-mark), the channel-well gradients, and the rgba() glows.

The label text is #15803D, not --brand-green, because green on its 8% tint is 2.1:1. #15803D gives 4.6:1. The live .toshi-visual-label has the same failure.

Motion — all under prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference

  • Flow streaks: stroke-dasharray 10 14, 1.6s linear, staggered.
  • Signals, not static lines: the 9 nodes (6 connectors and 3 users) are scattered around the tower with no fixed wiring. On every landing the cube throws signals: a shockwave ring on the core, then 4 packets arc out from that flash on the core to random nodes, and each node glows as a packet arrives. Halfway through the bounce, 2 random nodes send packets back into the flash point. It's traffic in both directions, never the same route twice. Packets are moved with transforms only (Web Animations API), and each node's glow is a 0.7s box-shadow pulse. Measured over two bounces: at most 4 packets in flight, and 16 node hits.
  • Bounce: the Toshi cube and its K tile share one 3s keyframe: a 2px squash on landing, a rise of 24px, then settling back down. Measured in sync at 0 / 1.3 / −24 / 0px. The core's footprint glow flares at each landing.
  • Toshi = the logo only. There's no tile and no "Toshi" word. The K spins like a coin above the cube, one turn every 10s (rotateY, preserve-3d). The back face is a second K turned 180°, so the mark reads the right way round from both sides and is never mirrored. There are brief edge-on moments, as with any coin. A soft shadow on the cube's top face grounds it, and it bounces with the cube.
  • Screens: each of the cube's two side faces carries an inset white screen, mapped onto the face plane (matrix(1 ±.5 0 1 …)). On every landing both screens change together with a 0.25s dip, working through the 6 models as 3 pairs (Anthropic + OpenAI, Gemini + Grok, Kimi + Z.ai). The swap is driven by animationiteration, so it can't drift from the bounce. Model logos are skewed onto the face as screen content; the K stays upright.
  • With reduced motion: there's no bounce, no signals and no spin: the K faces forward. Anthropic and OpenAI show statically, and the "Runs on" row always names all six.

Build notes

  • A fixed 1120×600 stage is scaled to fit its container. Satellites are real HTML .toshi-node elements, so hover states and Blade components (<x-brand.whatsapp />) carry over directly.
  • Below 760px: use the landing's existing t-wrapped fallback. Hide the lines, and stack the channels, the tower and the roles vertically. At phone width the scaled stage's labels would be too small to read.
  • Keep role="img" with its <title>/<desc>. The streak paths are aria-hidden.
