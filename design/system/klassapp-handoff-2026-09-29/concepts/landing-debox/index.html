Free-floating redesign — Phase 2

Each section shows Before (the current card values, taken from landing-preview.css) next to After, on the section's real surface. The system, applied everywhere below:

  • Ledger rules replace card edges. Each item gets a 1px hairline above it, in the hero's own ledger-rule colour family (rgba(120,95,60,.14)). This is the scanning structure: the eye reads rows from the rules.
  • Depth moves to the icon, not the container. The icon tile keeps its tinted gradient and soft shadow (the .ka-node recipe), so each item has one anchor point.
  • Alignment and gap do the grouping. The grid gaps grow from 14px to about 28px, so the white space between items reads as separation.
  • The section's ambient layer is the backdrop. Green glow for Toshi, paper for the hero, alternating light and white elsewhere. Nothing new is invented.

What this doesn't touch

  • Contrast: body text is #64748B, at 4.76:1 on white and 4.55:1 on --brand-light. Amber note #B45309: 5.02:1. Both honesty lines are unchanged in wording and position.
  • Tap targets: each FAQ summary is 56px tall and spans the full width (the cards were 56px too).
  • Motion: the chevron rotation is the only transition added, and it's off under reduced motion. The orbit and tower internals are unchanged.
  • Stays boxed: the Provable pillar (dashed amber), device frames, screenshots, app mockups, the orchestration panel, and the navbar.
