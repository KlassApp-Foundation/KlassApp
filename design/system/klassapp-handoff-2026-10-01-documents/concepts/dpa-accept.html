Behaviour

  1. What's recorded (immutable): school, agreement version and its SHA-256, the user's account, the typed name and title, the accepted time (UTC, with the school's time zone shown), the IP address, the user agent, the method ("click-to-accept") and an acceptance ID.
  2. No reading gate: accepting doesn't require scrolling to the end. Scroll gates fail with screen readers and zoom. The tick wording makes the confirmation explicit instead.
  3. Errors: errors appear only after Accept agreement is pressed, with a summary at the top of the form and focus moved to it. The button is never disabled.
  4. Signed PDF: generated once, server-side, from the accepted version. It's stored with its own hash, emailed to the person who accepted, and downloadable from Settings → Agreements. It's never regenerated with different content.
