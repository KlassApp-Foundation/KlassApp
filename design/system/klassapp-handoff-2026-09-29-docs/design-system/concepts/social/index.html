Social templates

There are six post types in the master 1080 × 1350 format, and one type (the announcement) adapted to every channel size. All values are in real pixels at export size; the previews are scaled down. Everything uses the brand palette: paper #FAFAF5 or navy #0C1528 (the reversed wordmark's own plate colour), green #15803D for text, Sora and DM Sans. The rendered sizes were checked in this browser; nothing has been built in Canva yet. The full spec is in CANVA-SPECS.md.
