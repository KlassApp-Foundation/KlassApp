KlassApp quick start

Follow your child at school from WhatsApp. No app to install.

For parents
1

Get connected

Give the school your WhatsApp number. The school links it to your child.

2

Say hello

Send any message to the school's KlassApp number to see the menu.

3

Report card

Send report and the report card arrives as a PDF.

4

Fees

Send fees to see what you have paid and what is left.

5

Attendance

Send attendance to see this term's record.

6

Results

Send results for the latest published marks.

Your privacy

Messages are only between you and the school. Other parents can't see your number.

New phone number?

Tell the school office, so it can update your number.
