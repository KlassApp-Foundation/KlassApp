KlassApp quick start

Your daily and termly tasks in KlassApp.

For teachers
1

Log in

Go to app.klassapp.xyz. Use the email link your school sent to set your password.

2

Mark attendance

Menu, then Attendance, then Mark today. Everyone starts as present. Tap a name to mark it Absent or Late, then tap Save attendance.

3

Enter marks

Menu, then Marks. Choose the stream and the exam. Type each mark; it saves when you move to the next box.

4

See your students

Menu, then Classes. You see only the students you teach.

5

Homework

Menu, then Homework, then Add. Students and parents can see it.

6

Your timetable

Menu, then Timetable, for your lessons this week.

Class teacher or subject teacher?

Only the class teacher of a stream can mark its attendance. Subject teachers enter marks for their own subject.
