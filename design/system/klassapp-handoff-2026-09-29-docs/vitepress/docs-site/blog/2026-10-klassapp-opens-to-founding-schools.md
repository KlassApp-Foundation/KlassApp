# KlassApp opens to founding schools
<PostMeta />

An open education protocol for humans and agents. Today, we start working with our first founding schools.

## What KlassApp is
KlassApp is an education protocol that runs in the tools educationists already use. Admins manage school operations in Slack, teachers enter marks from spreadsheets, and parents receive their children's school updates on WhatsApp, all by chatting in natural language with Toshi, your school's AI assistant.

Under the chat sits a full school system: students, classes and streams, attendance, marks, report cards and fees. It works for schools from nursery to secondary, with each school's own classes, terms and currency.

## Who can join
Any school, from nursery to secondary, wherever it is. You need a school admin who can set things up, and parents who use WhatsApp.

## How to join
1. Create your school account at [klassapp.xyz](https://klassapp.xyz/register).
2. Accept the data processing agreement.

## Open by design
The source is public on GitHub; supported self-hosting opens after an independent security review.
