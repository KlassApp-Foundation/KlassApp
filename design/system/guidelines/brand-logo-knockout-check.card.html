In both official source files the enclosed counters of A, p, p and a are not holes — they are separate paths painted the background-plate colour, because each export was flattened over its plate. Remove the plate and they become solid blobs. Every derived file here merges those four paths into their parent glyph with fill-rule="evenodd", making them real transparency. If any counter reads as a filled blob above, the merge failed for that glyph. The dark variants use the same fix and are checked on a light ground instead, since their “Klass” is #FEFEFE.
