The two real treatments

Avatar
.toshi-pill-avatar

38px, border-radius:50%, transparent fill, 1px var(--d-green) ring, overflow:hidden; mark inside at 24px (.toshi-pill-logo). Use this wherever Toshi appears as a participant — chat bubbles, message threads, the collapsed pill.

Toshi

24px mark at radius 2, no container, beside the name in Sora 700/15px (.toshi-header-logo). Use this for panel chrome, not for identity in a conversation.

It's a circle, and that's safe. Measured, the mark's ink reaches only 0.909 of its half-width, so at 24px in a 38px circle it clears the clip by 8.1px — and by 1.7px even drawn edge-to-edge. Don't switch to a square fearing a crop.

Needs box-sizing:border-box. The real class declares width:38px plus a 1px border and renders 38px total, because production gets a global border-box reset from Tailwind — which this bundle does not ship. Without the reset it renders 40px.
