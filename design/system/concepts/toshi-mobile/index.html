Toshi on phones

Admin role first, as a pattern every role reuses. Frames are real widths (375, 414, 768) running toshi.html, a click-through concept: open, close (✕, Esc, Back, swipe down on the handle), switch views, open the mode menu. Desktop (1280 and up) is unchanged: the right-hand dock with logo, "Toshi", mode menu, restart, maximise and close.

1 · Launcher

The bottom safe zone is the token --d-bottom-zone. It is --d-safe-bottom (max(16px, env(safe-area-inset-bottom))) on ordinary pages. On any page with a sticky action bar or footer, the layout sets <body data-sticky-bar> and the zone grows by --d-sticky-bar-h (68px). Nothing floating may sit inside it. It is drawn in red hatching below.

A · Labelled button in the top bar Recommended

  • Can’t overlap anything: it lives in the 56px top bar, outside the bottom zone entirely.
  • Labelled “Toshi”, so it explains itself; one tap from every page.
  • Quiet in preview: outline only, no badge, no motion.
  • Cost: the top bar holds 4 items at 375 (menu, title, Toshi, account); the title truncates first.

B · Bottom bar item

  • Easy thumb reach.
  • But KlassApp has no bottom navigation today, so this adds a whole new nav pattern. It also takes 56px from every page, and sticky form bars have to stack above it.
  • Worth it only if a bottom nav is introduced for other reasons.

C · Edge tab that hides on scroll

  • Small, and it sits above the bottom zone (here 12px above the form bar).
  • But it’s an icon with no label, so it doesn’t explain itself. It covers the right edge of content (table columns, menus), and it moves.
  • It’s the closest thing to the old pill and the zero-width toggle, both of which failed.

2 · The sheet on a phone

A full-height sheet (100dvh minus --d-sheet-top-gap) with a drag handle and a 44px close button. Not a full-screen takeover: the 8px gap and the scrim show that the page is still there underneath.

Closing: swipe down on the handle or header (over 120px closes; less springs back), ✕, Esc, or the browser Back button (opening pushes one history entry).

While open: role="dialog" aria-modal, focus moves to Close and is trapped, the page behind can’t scroll (body{overflow:hidden}), and scrolling inside the sheet doesn’t pass to the page (overscroll-behavior:contain). On close, focus returns to whatever opened the sheet.

Motion: a 240ms slide up with cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1). Under reduced motion it appears without the slide.

3a · Preview mode

For every school until Toshi ships. A “Coming soon” card with the agreed description, word for word, and a disabled composer with its reason. No chips, no pitch, no feature list.

The mode menu shows Setup guide and Assistant as not available, so the modes are discoverable without being promised.

3b · Setup guide (onboarding)

Two views of the same session, switched with a segmented control under the header: Checklist (the 7 setup steps with progress, shared with “Continue setup”) and Conversation. Tapping a step opens it in the conversation.

The conversation includes Yes/No confirm chips, plan cards (radio cards), a review card (nothing is saved until “Save fees”), a name-list upload card, and quick-reply chips above the composer.

3c · Assistant (early access, later)

“Early access” appears only in this mode: in the header pill and on the launcher. The screen has free chat, tool results as cards with their source, a typing indicator, and an approval card for anything that changes records or sends messages. The card lists what will change, with Reject and Approve and send.

4 · States

Keyboard open: the sheet’s bottom follows visualViewport, so the composer sits on top of the keyboard and the messages scroll above it. This is shown with a 291px keyboard.

Long messages: they wrap with overflow-wrap:anywhere and never widen the sheet.

5 · 768 tablet

A side sheet, not the desktop dock. It is 400px wide (--d-toshi-sheet-w), full height, slides in from the right over a scrim, and has no drag handle (close with ✕, Esc or Back). The page keeps its width behind it.

Breakpoints: the bottom sheet up to 760px, the side sheet from 761 to 1279px, and the desktop dock from 1280px (the existing toshi-ui.css rule, unchanged). 760px is the admin shell’s own breakpoint, where the sidebar becomes a drawer, so the two change together.

Launcher at 761–1279: the app shows the sidebar here and has no top bar, so the launcher is a 44px “Toshi” row in the sidebar, above the account card. This concept draws a top bar at 768 only to keep the frame simple.

Rules

  • Toshi never opens by itself: no auto-open on first login, after setup, on errors or on any page load.
  • Toshi off: no launcher, no setup-card button, no sheet markup and no toshi-ui.css (profiles handoff Part C).
  • Copy: the description is used only on the preview card, word for word. “Early access” appears only in assistant mode. Nothing describes features that aren’t live.

What the current phone experience gets wrong

  1. No way in: below 1280px the edge toggle is a zero-width sliver, so on a phone the only way to open Toshi is the setup card’s “Set up with Toshi” button. Once that card is hidden or setup is done, Toshi is unreachable.
  2. The old pill covered things: the floating “Toshi Agent · Talk” pill covered footer actions on teacher pages, which is why it was removed. Nothing reserved space for sticky bars, and there was no bottom safe-zone token.
  3. Undefined panel below 1280: it falls back to the old floating widget from the main stylesheet, and goes full-screen only at 640px and below. There is no defined tablet layout.
  4. No phone behaviour: no swipe to close, the Back button leaves the page instead of closing Toshi, the page behind still scrolls, and focus isn’t trapped.
  5. Promotion in preview: while Toshi is in preview, it is still promoted on the dashboard (the Toshi carousel and the open side panel with a disabled message box) instead of a quiet, single entry point.
