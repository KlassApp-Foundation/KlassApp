These layouts borrow patterns from the TinyFish console: small "Welcome back" over a large title, one dismissible banner, cards on the left with a snapshot on the right, grouped sidebar labels, a setup chip and a split sign-up page. Icons are Lucide (lucide@0.460.0), per the icon spec in concepts/icons/. The brand is KlassApp throughout: paper canvas, #15803D green, Sora and DM Sans, the KlassApp logo, 44px targets and 4.5:1 text. Contrast is calculated from hex, not browser-measured.
