Icons: one set, Lucide

Read at main @ 3cf3025bf5b0. Today the app mixes three sets:

  • Sidebar: x-icons.sidebar, hand-copied Feather glyphs at stroke 2 with a duotone fill layer.
  • Dashboard and KPI cards: x-ds-kpi-card, Heroicons v1 outline.
  • Old icon files: 44 legacy resources/assets/icons/*.svg files from the church product, including church, baptism and e-prayer icons.

Lucide is the maintained fork of Feather, so the sidebar glyphs move over with almost no visual change. The right-hand column below is drawn live from lucide@0.460.0.

Checking every Lucide name against the library…

Sizes

16 is for dense tables, badges and inline text. 20 is the default for nav, buttons, list rows and inputs. 24 is for empty states (inside a 48px box) and page headers. The icon is never the tap target: its button or row stays at least 44px.

Stroke

Stroke is 2 at 16 and 20, and 1.75 at 24 and up. Caps and joins are round. absoluteStrokeWidth is off. No fills and no duotone: the current icon-fill-targets layer is dropped, and the active nav state comes from the row background and weight.

Colour tokens

Icons are always currentColor. Any icon that carries meaning needs at least 3:1 against its background. #94A3B8 (2.56:1 on white) is retired for icons.
