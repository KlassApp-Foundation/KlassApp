1. Purpose and scope

This agreement applies whenever the School uses KlassApp to record, store or share personal data about students, parents, guardians and staff. It forms part of the KlassApp Terms of Service.

1.1 What KlassApp processes

  • Identity and contact details entered by the School.
  • Academic records: classes, attendance, marks and report cards.
  • Fee records and payment references.
  • Health and support notes, only where the School chooses to record them.

1.2 What KlassApp does not do

KlassApp does not sell personal data, use it for advertising, or use it for any purpose the School has not instructed.

3. Security

KlassApp keeps each school's data separate, encrypts it in transit and at rest, and limits staff access to what is needed to run the service.

Note

Health and support notes are visible only to staff the School authorises, and each view is logged.

4. Breach notification

KlassApp will tell the School without undue delay, and within 72 hours, after becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting the School's data.
